Services We Offer

We provide custom real estate consultancy and standard market feasibility studies for real estate projects. If you are site selecting, developing or building ground-up projects from smaller sites to MPC and 1000+ acreage mixed-use projects, we work across the real estate asset types of single-family, multifamily, office, retail, industrial and other special uses to determine highest and best use, feasibility and demand.

We are your third-party real estate market data solution for demand and absorption market studies that help you market the project, for government approvals, bond issuance, or for analysis and product mix. We are experienced in producing studies for MUD, MMD, PID or any special tax or bond district that satisfies state requirements such as TCEQ approval in Texas.

Our focus is on the real estate and the ecosystem of the built world. We work with your partners who financially model, or other engineering and real estate partners, to ensure that we understand the site feasibility constraints.

Market Studies and Feasibility Analysis Services:

  • City and MSA level analysis
  • Drivers of job and population growth
  • Overview of Housing and Retail in the CMA
  • Competitive Market Area analysis
  • Asset comparables
  • Demand and Absorption forecasting
  • Determine the highest and best use
