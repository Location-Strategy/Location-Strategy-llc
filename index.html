Services We Offer

We provide custom real estate advisory and market feasibility studies for projects of every scale—from individual sites to master-planned communities and mixed-use developments of 1,000 acres or more. Across single-family, multifamily, office, retail, industrial, and specialty uses, we evaluate highest and best use, feasibility, and demand.

We are an independent source of market data for demand and absorption studies used in project marketing, government approvals, bond issuance, and product-mix decisions. We prepare studies for MUDs, MMDs, PIDs, and other special tax or bond districts, including work that meets state requirements such as TCEQ approval in Texas.

Our work is grounded in real estate and the broader built environment. We coordinate with financial modelers, engineers, and other project partners so site constraints are reflected in the analysis.
