About Us

We specialize in real estate advisory grounded in transactional experience and thorough market analysis. We work across the United States. Sound strategy takes more than a spreadsheet. It takes understanding the why behind the what.

We use a data-driven approach to measure performance, then apply a practical reading of how people use places. Those differences show up in vacancy, rents, sale prices, and turnover—and they often explain why one asset outperforms another.

Data has limits. Relationships fill the gaps. We talk with industry, government, and economic-development professionals for market color. From commercial developments to residential portfolios, we deliver the insight needed to navigate a complex real estate market.

Principal

For more than a decade, Julie Chang worked on Wall Street with buy-side and sell-side clients in market data, technology, and analytics.

As a licensed California real estate agent, she saw how emotion shapes residential and multifamily decisions. She brings that perspective to her analytical work: data still leads, but people determine how an asset performs. Real estate is built for occupants, not spreadsheets.

Julie is a graduate of the University of Texas at Austin and a Texas native. Travel and time spent in different cities sharpened her interest in how the built environment shapes community, walkability, the workforce, and everyday satisfaction. Desirability is easy to understate and hard to ignore in financial results. Once you control for location, why do two similar assets perform differently? We keep building the analytics that answer that question for institutional clients developing the next generation of real estate.
